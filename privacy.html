Swipe Clean Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the Swipe Clean mobile app (“Swipe Clean,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information. Swipe Clean helps you review photos and videos already in your device’s media library.

Photos and videos

With your permission, Swipe Clean accesses photo and video library items so it can display them, organize them for review, and let you choose items to keep or delete. The app reads media identifiers and basic metadata such as media type, creation time, dimensions, and video duration. Swipe Clean does not upload your photos or videos to us or to Swipe Clean servers.

When you choose to delete items, the app asks the device’s media library to perform the deletion. Your device may show a system confirmation before it completes. You can control or revoke media access in your device settings.

Information stored on your device

Swipe Clean saves the identifiers of items you marked to keep or move to the in-app trash. This review state is stored locally on your device using the app’s local storage. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove this saved review state. Device backup features may handle app data according to your device and account settings.

Information collection and sharing

Swipe Clean does not operate an account system, show ads, use analytics, or send your library information to us. We do not sell or share personal information collected by the app because the app does not collect personal information for those purposes.

Voluntary support link

The app includes an optional link to Buy Me a Coffee. If you open it, you leave Swipe Clean and interact with a third-party service. That service may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not receive your payment credentials through the app. See the Buy Me a Coffee privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Swipe Clean is a general utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and revise the date above. Please review this page from time to time.

Contact
